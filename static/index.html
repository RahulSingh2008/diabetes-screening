Helpful to know

Common risk factors

Risk can be influenced by many things. Some common factors associated with type 2 diabetes include:

  • ✓ A close family member with diabetes
  • ✓ Being physically inactive
  • ✓ A history of high blood pressure
  • ✓ A history of gestational diabetes

Having a risk factor does not mean you have diabetes.

About this screening tool

Prediction API contract POST /api/predict accepts the eight input fields and returns {"probability":0.0,"prediction":0,"risk":"Low","threshold":0.5}. Batch requests use POST /api/predict/batch with {"records":[...]} and return {"threshold":0.5,"results":[...]}. Probability and threshold values are fractions from 0 to 1.

Decision threshold and limitations

The selected threshold is returned by the prediction server and marked on the probability gauge. A missed diabetic may be more harmful than a false alarm, so threshold selection should consider the consequences of missed cases alongside false alarms and clinical review.

The Pima Indians Diabetes Database represents women aged 21 and older. Model performance may not generalize to other populations. Results depend on input quality and require independent clinical evaluation.
